WHAT CAN YOU DO?


How can ordinary people like you and I come together in order to make an impact?


Support the Right Creators

Unfollow and avoid engaging with child creators or family vlogging channels. Without the engagement, abusive industries like these can't survive. Instead, support creators who blur the faces of minors, or exclude children from their content altogether.

Report Dangerous Behavior

If you come across channels or accounts that appear to be exploiting kids, make sure you report it immediately.

Spread the Word

There are plenty of content creators who speak out against child influencers. However, you dont have to post videos to make a change. Educate your friends and family, spread petitions, and urge others to join you in your mission.

Contact Local Lawmakers

Enforcing laws surrounding child content creation is a large goal, but one that will protect thousands of childrens childhoods. On top of signing petitions, send emails to your local lawmakers about your concerns. Your voice matters, and it's the first step to change.
